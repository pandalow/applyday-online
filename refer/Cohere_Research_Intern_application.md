## Cover Letter

Dear Cohere Hiring Team,

I am excited to apply for the Research Internship. Cohere's mission to scale intelligence through frontier language models strongly matches my research interests in NLP, multimodal learning, retrieval-augmented generation, evaluation, and applied AI systems.

I recently completed an MSc in Software Design and Development at the University of Galway with First Class Honours. My final-year project, which received the School of Computer Science Best Thesis Award, was a knowledge-augmented multimodal framework for detecting and countering harmful memes. In this project, I built ToxiLEN, a multimodal framework for implicit misogyny detection and counter-narrative generation, achieving a Macro F1 of 95.04% and outperforming strong state-of-the-art baselines.

The work combined several areas that align closely with Cohere's research directions. I designed a background knowledge enhancement module extending CLIP with span-aware mechanisms and external RoBERTa knowledge; built a hybrid retrieval pipeline using BM25 and BGE-ZH dense embeddings with Pyserini and FAISS; implemented diverse example retrieval and in-context learning to reduce hallucinations and output toxicity; and evaluated GPT-4o, Llama-3.3, and DeepSeek-R1 with automated metrics and human evaluation. I also helped create a human-in-the-loop curated dataset by combining LLM outputs with expert annotations.

Beyond the thesis, I have built applied NLP and LLM systems. ApplyDay uses LangChain, spaCy, scikit-learn, and TF-IDF to analyze job descriptions, extract keywords, compare resumes, and visualize application analytics. INFO Spark Copilot is a browser-based AI writing assistant using local Gemini Nano inference, real-time streaming, context-aware page perception, and prompt templates. These projects taught me to think not only about model capability, but also about evaluation, user trust, latency, privacy, and how AI systems behave when placed in front of users.

I understand that Cohere's Research Internship is primarily designed for PhD candidates, and I would be applying as an exceptional non-PhD candidate. What I can offer is a focused NLP/LLM research portfolio, strong implementation ability in Python and PyTorch-based tooling, experience with retrieval and evaluation pipelines, and an applied mindset shaped by building real AI systems and prior product work at Meituan, Alibaba, and ByteDance.

I would be especially excited to contribute to teams working on retrieval-augmented generation, data and evaluation, safety, multimodal models, or applied NLP. Thank you for considering my application.

Kind regards,  
Xiaojian Zhuang
